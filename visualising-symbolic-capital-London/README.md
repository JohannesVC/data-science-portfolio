
## Visualising Urban Change and Symbolic Capital in London

Analyses the spatial relationship between Low-Traffic Neighbourhoods (LTNs) and “symbolic capital” retail in London (e.g. artisanal cafes).

### Key features:
- GeoPandas, Voronoi diagrams, road-network analysis
- Retail clustering and income-demographics overlays
- Great example of urban data storytelling
